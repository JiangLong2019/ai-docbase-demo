Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI Documentation Base?

An AI Documentation Base is a centralized knowledge repository that uses artificial intelligence to organize, search, and retrieve information efficiently. It helps teams maintain up-to-date documentation and provides intelligent assistance for finding relevant information.

Key features include:

  • Intelligent search capabilities
  • Auto-categorization of content
  • Content recommendations
  • Version control and tracking
How do I get started with the platform?

Getting started is simple and can be completed in just a few steps:

  1. Sign up for an account on our platform
  2. Configure your workspace and team settings
  3. Import your existing documentation or start creating new content
  4. Customize the AI settings to match your organization's needs

Our onboarding team will guide you through the setup process and provide training for your team members.

What file formats are supported?

Our platform supports a wide variety of file formats to ensure compatibility with your existing documentation:

  • Text formats: .txt, .md, .doc, .docx, .rtf
  • Presentation formats: .ppt, .pptx, .key
  • PDF documents: .pdf with text extraction capabilities
  • Web formats: .html, .xml
  • Spreadsheets: .xls, .xlsx, .csv

If you have content in other formats, contact our support team for assistance with custom import solutions.

Is my data secure and private?

Data security and privacy are our top priorities. We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption
  • Access Control: Role-based permissions and multi-factor authentication
  • Compliance: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and ISO 27001 certified
  • Data Residency: Choose where your data is stored geographically
  • Regular Audits: Independent security assessments and penetration testing

Your data remains yours - we never use it to train our models or share it with third parties.

Can I integrate with existing tools?

Yes! Our platform offers extensive integration capabilities with popular productivity and development tools:

  • Collaboration Tools: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord
  • Development: GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Confluence
  • Documentation: Notion, Obsidian, Roam Research
  • Cloud Storage: Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive
  • API Access: RESTful APIs for custom integrations

Our technical team can help you set up custom integrations if your preferred tool isn't listed.
